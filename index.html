Selected work

Chinese calligraphy · Ink on paper

Ode to Guangxi

Inspired by Guangxi, this piece explores how brush pressure and spacing give written characters a visual rhythm. The two photographs show the brush at work and the shifts in weight and texture visible in the finished strokes.

Chinese calligraphy · Ink on coloured paper

Chán 禪

This single-character composition centres on chán, the Chinese term for Zen. A long vertical stroke anchors the character, while dry-brush edges reveal the warm paper beneath. Two red seals punctuate the composition.

After brushing the main character, I added the smaller inscription in the open space to the side, keeping the large character as the focus.

Visual identity · Rhino 3D modelling

Daya Visual Identity

I began with a hand-drawn study of a letter D crossed by a tilted orbit. I simplified the curves and star points, then built a three-dimensional version in Rhino. The gold renders show how the depth and intersections change as the mark turns.
